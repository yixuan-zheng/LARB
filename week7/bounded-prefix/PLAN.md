# Bounded-prefix offline screen

Use one read at the 4,096-token checkpoint, with the original question and the last 512 saved generator token IDs decoded using the pinned generator tokenizer. Use a local 4-bit Qwen3-4B reader in non-thinking mode. In one call, score four possible correctness outcomes using normalized next-token probabilities, then generate a short evidence note from the same cached prompt, with at most 96 output tokens including the outcome label. Rank by P(wrong/correct) minus P(correct/wrong). Treat these as uncalibrated forecasts. No prompt or model selection uses outcomes.

Run a matched local question-only control once per question, with the same instructions and empty prefix, and reuse its prediction across seeds. Also retain the historical Week 6 question-only ranking. Use randomized opaque IDs and read packets without grades, original IDs, seed, or old labels. Seal forecasts and costs before scoring. All 30 questions are development data.

Report half allocation (28 extensions among 57 eligible prefixes) and the ranked top-k frontier with all counts and uniform ties. Include shared prefixes once in each hypothetical serving cost. Charge the local reader on every eligible request for both policies, even though the offline question-only forecast is collected only once per question. Price reader input/output at one generator-token equivalent each as the declared accounting scenario, with sensitivity at 0, 0.25, 0.5, 1 and 2. Charge the full bounded read against the archival question-only comparison because archival read costs were unmeasured. Record local warm wall time separately. Online synchronization and concurrent service overhead require later measurements.

Primary comparison uses the archival question-only 28-extension output cost as the ceiling, with at least two additional correct answers required at that ceiling after accounting. Require lower cost than archival question-only to recover fixed-long accuracy, and a strict gain-seed win on at least three of four discordant questions. Use all ranked operating points to describe these offline frontiers. The costs are realized after grading and cannot set an online decision per request. Record break-even reader costs at equal accuracy. Bootstrap paired questions with their seeds together, holding the ranked half allocation fixed and rebuilding its ranks within each resample. A failed point gate stops this candidate. An otherwise passing result with a nonpositive lower confidence bound for the matched-extension accuracy delta is inconclusive. A pass earns review of a protected evaluation and its overhead budget.
