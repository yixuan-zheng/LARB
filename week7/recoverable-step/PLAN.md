# Protected recoverable-step test

At 4,096 reasoning tokens, a reader required to perform one concrete operation will allocate 4K→8K continuation better than direct outcome forecasting. For a positive result the proposed policy must also beat question-only after charging observation. The reader's step is never injected into the served generator.

Use all 30 MathArena AIME 2026 questions, with two paired prefix seeds per question. Pin the public dataset revision and compare normalized text to prior workspace inputs. Keep every question and both seeds together for uncertainty. Protected means unused for LARB signal development. Generation retains the Qwen3-4B BF16 revision, pinned vLLM stack, sampling, cue, answer cap and integer grader from Week 6. Preserve shared prefixes, sample distinct seeded suffixes, and include naturally early answers identically. Require complete raw coverage and successful engineering gates before scoring. Freeze the actual GPU type and resource envelope in the staged source lock.

Use the same pinned local Qwen3-4B 4-bit reader as the bounded-prefix screen. Provide the question and exact final 512 generator token IDs decoded to text, one call with at most 96 generated tokens. Ask first whether the requested answer already follows. Otherwise require an unmet requirement, verbatim supporting excerpt, and the result of performing one concrete operation. Parse the final decision as READY, STEP, or INSUFFICIENT. A missing/malformed answer or unsupported evidence quote is INSUFFICIENT. Use no outcome or answer key in the reader packets. Collect a same-reader question-only direct forecast once per question and an old-prompt direct forecast once per eligible prefix. Seal all forecasts before grading. Primary ordering prioritizes STEP, then INSUFFICIENT, then READY. Within each class use the question-only net forecast, with uniform ties. The diagnostic control ranks by old-prompt P(wrong/correct) minus P(correct/wrong).

Predeclare the retrospective cost frontier as all ranked top-k allocations with uniform ties. The common ceiling is the realized generation cost of the half-allocation question-only policy plus its reader cost. Compare each policy's maximum expected correctness within that ceiling, including the question-only control's own full frontier. Reader costs count input and output tokens per eligible request, including question-only on both seeds, at one generator-token equivalent each. Report cost sensitivity at 0, 0.25, 0.5, 1 and 2 and warm latency separately. These retrospective frontier calculations do not set a deployable per-request spending rule. Count the shared prefix once in a selected branch cost, and report paid prefills/suffixes separately as experimental overhead.

Advance only if the candidate delivers at least two more expected correct answers than question-only at the primary charged ceiling, and the paired question bootstrap 95% interval for that difference in accuracy fractions excludes zero on the positive side. Recompute the ranks, question-only half-allocation ceiling and frontier within each of 10,000 resamples. If fewer than four eligible pairs have discordant correctness, call the study low-information. Report same-count accuracy, gains, losses, fixed-budget endpoints, and old-prompt diagnostic even if the primary gate fails. Audit up to ten STEP claims selected by stable hash and up to five READY claims before outcome join for evidence relevance, operation validity and advancement. Findings from that audit cannot change the frozen ranking.

An advance result supports serving feasibility review. Concurrent goodput and synchronization latency remain outside this isolated paired study.
