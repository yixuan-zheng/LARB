# Frozen correctness replication

Use all 115 eligible archived Week 6/7 prefixes and their existing 8K-policy final correctness labels. No new decoding and no Experiment 2 labels. Keep input IDs and features separate from grades until scoring. The final normalized layer's last four reasoning tokens are pooled at each Temporal checkpoint, plus 4K. Prompt-only is the final prompt token, and the final 4K token is saved as a separate control. Replay exact IDs in BF16, evaluation mode, SDPA, one sequence at a time. The original paper used different models, data, decoding and split. This is a methodological replication.

Before bulk, compare three length-spanning examples at all checkpoints against the pinned upstream helper with exact prompt IDs and last-position logits. Require finite nonzero features, matching shapes, relative L2 error at most .02, cosine at least .999, at most 36 GiB peak reserved memory, and conservative projected bulk replay at most 40 minutes. Use one L40S with a one-hour allocation. The gate measures rather than assumes prefill throughput. Full forwards process approximately 0.5M archived input tokens. Record all checks and stop on failure.

Use five deterministic shuffled question-group folds at seed 356, with both seeds grouped and exact normalized duplicate questions merged. Fit PCA only on training rows, up to 128 components and training sample rank, then upstream L2 logistic regression (C=1, liblinear, balanced classes, 1,000 iterations). Report pooled out-of-fold AUROC, accuracy, Brier and cohort counts. Primary contrasts are t=4 and t=4096 versus prompt-only. Bootstrap paired held-out predictions by question with 10,000 replicates and fixed fitted probes. These intervals are conditional on the fitted folds and do not capture training variability. Report the full temporal curve, 4K single-token control, and highest-confidence 4K misses by false-positive and false-negative direction. No threshold tuning, layer selection, or continuation-benefit fit. The cohort is selected for 4K survival and the target is a single archived 8K outcome. Correctness discrimination does not establish predictability of p8 minus p4.
